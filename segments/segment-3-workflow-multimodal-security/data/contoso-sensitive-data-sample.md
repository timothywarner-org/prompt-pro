## Why This Matters

If any of the above data were real, pasting it into a public AI tool would constitute:

1. **A data breach** -- SSNs and financial account numbers are personally identifiable information (PII) protected by federal and state law.
2. **A HIPAA concern** -- health insurance enrollment tied to named individuals is protected health information (PHI).
3. **An employment law risk** -- performance review excerpts shared externally could expose the company to wrongful termination or discrimination claims.
4. **A financial fraud vector** -- bank routing and account numbers are sufficient for initiating unauthorized ACH transfers.

### What to Do Instead

- Use enterprise-tier AI tools with data processing agreements (ChatGPT Enterprise, Claude Team/Enterprise, M365 Copilot).
- Anonymize data before using it with any AI tool: replace names with "Employee A," redact SSNs, mask account numbers.
- Never paste raw database exports, HR records, or financial data into a consumer-tier AI chat.
- Establish a company policy that defines what data classifications are permitted in AI tools at each tier.
