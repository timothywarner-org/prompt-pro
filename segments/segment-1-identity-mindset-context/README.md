# Segment 1: Identity, Mindset & Context Foundations

**Duration:** 50 minutes | **Level:** Intermediate | **Last Updated:** June 2026

## Overview

This segment introduces Warner's Laws 1-7 -- the foundational principles that shape how you think about AI before you ever type a prompt. Using the **Contoso Robotics** scenario, learners explore responsibility, identity, cognitive traps, and the critical role of context.

---

## Warner's Laws Covered

| Law | Title | Core Idea |
|-----|-------|-----------|
| 1 | Pilot/Co-pilot Responsibility | You are the pilot; the AI is your co-pilot. Final decisions are yours. |
| 2 | Identity Awareness | Your signed-in identity determines what data and capabilities the AI can access. |
| 3 | Anchor Trap | Reading AI output before forming your own opinion biases your judgment. |
| 4 | Trusting Your Gut | Domain expertise still matters -- use AI to augment, not replace, your instincts. |
| 5 | Prompt Smell | If a prompt feels wrong, it probably is. Learn to recognize the warning signs. |
| 6 | Context Disclosure | The more relevant context you provide, the better the output. |
| 7 | Inference from Omission | AI will fill gaps in your prompt with assumptions -- sometimes wrong ones. |

---

## Scenario: Contoso Robotics

All demos and exercises use a single fictional company:

- **Company:** Contoso Robotics
- **Headquarters:** Austin, TX
- **CEO:** Maria Chen
- **Employees:** 500
- **Annual Revenue:** $120M
- **Products:** WarehouseBot Pro, WarehouseBot Lite, LogiMover 500
- **Strategic Move:** Expanding from warehouse automation into healthcare logistics

See `data/contoso-company-profile.md` for the full company profile learners will paste into prompts.
