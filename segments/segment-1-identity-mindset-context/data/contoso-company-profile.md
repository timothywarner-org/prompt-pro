# Contoso Robotics -- Company Profile

**Founded:** 2018 | **Headquarters:** Austin, TX | **CEO:** Maria Chen

Contoso Robotics designs and manufactures autonomous robots for warehouse and logistics operations. The company employs 500 people across engineering, manufacturing, sales, and support functions, with regional offices in London, Singapore, and Sao Paulo.

**Annual Revenue:** $120M (FY2025)

**Products:**

- **WarehouseBot Pro** -- Flagship autonomous warehouse robot with advanced navigation, heavy payload capacity (up to 500 kg), and multi-floor operation. Priced at $85K per unit.
- **WarehouseBot Lite** -- Mid-market model for small and mid-size distribution centers. Payload capacity up to 200 kg. Priced at $42K per unit.
- **LogiMover 500** -- Compact logistics automation robot for last-mile sortation and internal transport. Priced at $28K per unit.

**Markets Served:** North America (60% of revenue), EMEA (25%), APAC (10%), LATAM (5%).

**Mission:** To make logistics safer, faster, and more affordable through intelligent automation.

**Strategic Priorities (2026):**

1. Expand into healthcare logistics robotics for hospital supply chain automation.
2. Grow APAC and LATAM revenue to 25% of total by 2028.
3. Launch a robotics-as-a-service (RaaS) subscription model.
4. Achieve ISO 13482 safety certification for all product lines.
