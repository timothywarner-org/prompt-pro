# Anchor Trap Exercise

**Law 3: The Anchor Trap** -- Reading AI output before forming your own opinion biases your judgment.

---

## The Business Question

> Should Contoso Robotics enter the healthcare logistics market?

**Background:** Contoso Robotics is a $120M warehouse automation company based in Austin, TX. They currently sell three products (WarehouseBot Pro, WarehouseBot Lite, LogiMover 500) across four regions. Leadership is evaluating whether to expand into healthcare logistics -- a regulated, high-growth market that demands precision, compliance, and reliability.

## Step 2: Prompt the AI (5 minutes)

Open ChatGPT, Copilot, or Gemini and run this prompt:

```text
You are a senior strategy consultant advising a mid-size robotics company.

Company: Contoso Robotics
- Headquarters: Austin, TX
- Employees: 500
- Revenue: $120M/year
- Current products: Warehouse automation robots (WarehouseBot Pro, WarehouseBot Lite, LogiMover 500)
- Regions: North America, EMEA, APAC, LATAM

Question: Should Contoso Robotics enter the healthcare logistics market?

Provide a structured analysis including:
1. Market opportunity assessment
2. Top 3 arguments for entry
3. Top 3 arguments against entry
4. Key risks and mitigation strategies
5. A clear recommendation with rationale

Keep the response under 500 words.
```

---

## Step 3: Compare and Reflect (10 minutes)

Now compare your draft with the AI output. Answer these questions honestly:

### Reflection Questions

1. **Anchoring check:** Did the AI raise any points you had not considered? Would those points have changed your recommendation if you had seen them first?

2. **Confidence shift:** Has your confidence level changed after reading the AI response? Did it go up or down, and why?

3. **Missing nuance:** Did your draft include any company-specific insights, gut feelings, or industry knowledge that the AI missed entirely?

4. **Reversal test:** If you had read the AI output first, would you have written the same draft -- or would the AI's framing have shaped your thinking?

5. **Decision quality:** Which analysis would you trust more for an actual board presentation -- yours, the AI's, or a combination? Why?

---

## Key Takeaway

The anchor trap is real. Once you read an AI-generated answer, it is very difficult to think independently about the same question. The discipline of writing your answer first protects the value of your own expertise and ensures the AI augments your thinking rather than replacing it.

This is why Law 3 exists: **form your opinion before you prompt.**
