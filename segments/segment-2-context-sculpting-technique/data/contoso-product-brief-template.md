# Contoso Robotics -- Product Brief Template

Use this template when writing product briefs in Contoso's house style. Every brief follows the same structure so that sales, marketing, and engineering can scan any product page and find the information they need in the same place every time.

---

## [Product Name]

**Tagline:** _A single sentence (under 12 words) that captures the product's core value proposition._

### Overview

Two to three short paragraphs (no more than 150 words total). Cover what the product does, who it is for, and why it matters. Write in active voice. Avoid jargon that a non-technical buyer would not understand.

### Key Specifications

| Spec | Value |
|------|-------|
| Payload capacity | _e.g., 500 kg_ |
| Top speed | _e.g., 2.0 m/s_ |
| Battery life | _e.g., 12 hours continuous_ |
| Navigation | _e.g., LiDAR + stereo vision_ |
| Software integration | _e.g., SAP, Oracle, Manhattan WMS_ |
| Safety rating | _e.g., ISO 3691-4 compliant_ |

Add or remove rows as needed, but always include payload, speed, battery life, and safety rating.

### Target Market

One paragraph. Name the industry segment, facility type, and ideal customer profile (e.g., company size, floor-space range).

### Competitive Advantage

One paragraph. State the primary differentiator with a quantified claim where possible (e.g., "40% faster pick-path optimization than nearest competitor").

### Pricing Tier

State the tier name and approximate per-unit range. Tiers used at Contoso:

| Tier | Typical Range |
|------|---------------|
| Entry | $20,000 - $45,000 |
| Mid-market | $42,000 - $70,000 |
| Enterprise | $75,000 - $150,000 |
| Custom | Quote required |

---

## Style Notes

- Sentence case for all headings.
- No exclamation marks.
- Numbers: use digits for anything above nine; spell out one through nine.
- Units: metric first, imperial in parentheses if relevant.
- Keep the brief to a single page when printed at 11 pt font.
